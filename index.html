Goodmeetings FAQ

WHAT IS GOODMEETINGS?

Goodmeetings is a modern video conferencing and meeting platform designed for teams, sales, and customer-facing organizations. It helps you host, join, and manage meetings, share updates, and collaborate — all in one place.

DO I NEED ANYTHING SPECIAL TO USE IT?

No special setup is required. All you need is an internet connection and a computer or mobile device. Create your account, invite your team, and you’re ready to go. No downloads or complex configuration needed.

HOW CAN I SEND UPDATES TO MY TEAM?

You can share updates during a meeting, or use the built-in chat and screen sharing features. You can also share meeting notes and recordings with your team after the call.

HOW CAN I ENABLE NIGHT MODE?

To enable night mode, go to your profile settings and switch to Dark Mode. This will automatically adjust the interface to a darker theme, making it easier on your eyes during low-light hours.
