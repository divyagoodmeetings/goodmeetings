THE MANDATE

From best practice to
binding mandate.

The RBI, effective July 1 2026, has moved collection call conduct from best practice to binding mandate. Contact hours, tone, and third party disclosure are no longer grey areas. Lenders are now required to run real time monitoring on every recovery interaction.

Goodmeetings clients are already positioned ahead of this shift.
